# Using Site Monitor

This guide is for owners and operators who watch site health. Every step uses the labels you see on screen.

## What you're looking at

| Screen or signal | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Site monitor** dashboard | The overall picture of your checks |
| A passing **Check** | That part of the site is healthy |
| A failing **Check** | Something needs attention |
| **Last checked** / **Next check** | When a check ran and when it runs again |

## What to do when...

| Situation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A check is failing | Open the check to see what it found, and act on the detail it gives |
| You want a fresh result now | Use **Run due checks** instead of waiting for the schedule |
| You're unsure what a check means | Note what it monitors; if it needs technical work, share it with your developer |
| Everything is green | Nothing to do; check back as part of your routine |

## Settings and retention

- Checks run on a schedule; you don't need to run them by hand for normal monitoring.
- Use **Run due checks** when you want an immediate result after a change.
